HOW TO PLAY

  • Draw a route. Press a plane, drag a path, release. It flies the path you drew.
  • Colours match. Send each plane to the runway in its own colour — land on the wrong one and it goes around.
  • Finish the drag on the glowing ring at the outer end of the runway. That is the landing clearance — the route locks onto the approach line and the callsign gets a tick. A plane whose route ends anywhere else will fly over the strip and go around.
  • Redraw anytime. Touch a plane again to replace its route.
  • Keep them apart. A red circle means two planes are closing in. Curve one away.
  • Don't let planes leave the airspace — a lost aircraft costs a life, same as a crash.
